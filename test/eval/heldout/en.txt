=== news ===
The city council voted on Tuesday to extend the opening hours of the public library, after a petition signed by more than two thousand residents. Under the new schedule, the main branch will stay open until nine in the evening on weekdays and until five on Saturdays. Council members said the change would cost about forty thousand dollars a year, which will be covered by the existing culture budget. Several students told reporters that they often struggle to find quiet places to study at night, and that the longer hours would make a real difference. The library director welcomed the decision and promised to hire two additional part-time employees before the start of the autumn term.
